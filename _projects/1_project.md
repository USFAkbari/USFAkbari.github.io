### Overview

Designed and implemented an automated computer vision pipeline for detecting and classifying planets in astronomical imagery from publicly available NASA and ESA archives as part of Bachelor's degree studies at Azad University, North Tehran Branch.

### Technical Highlights
- **Preprocessing:** Applied Gaussian and median filtering, histogram equalization, CLAHE, and background subtraction.
- **Feature Extraction:** Extracted morphological, photometric, color-histogram, and Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) texture features.
- **Classification:** Evaluated and trained Support Vector Machines (SVM) and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classifiers for multi-class planetary identification.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, OpenCV, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib.
